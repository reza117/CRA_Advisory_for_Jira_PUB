2. What the App does

The App helps a manufacturer track the CRA Article 14(8) early-warning and 72-hour clocks, prepare Single Reporting Platform fields, and draft a customer advisory. The customer advisory export is oriented to CSAF 2.0. The CSIRT dossier is a PDF of the saved fields. The App does not file a notice with a regulator.

3. Data processed

Category Examples Where stored
CRA case Issue id and key, project, track, awareness time, deadlines Forge Storage on your Jira site
Report pack The fields you type for the 24-hour, 72-hour, and final notices Forge Storage
Customer advisory Audience, TLP, affected versions, mitigation, CWE id Forge Storage
Product registry Product name, version, and the other product fields you save Forge Storage
Escalation roster Jira account id and display name Forge Storage. Names are read with read:jira-user
Audit log Awareness and correction rows: time, track, and actor account id Forge Storage
Jira issue context Issue id, issue key, and project Read with read:jira-work. Not copied off your site

The App does not send this data to the publisher. It has no publisher server, no analytics, and no public fetch.

4. Purpose

  • Show the clocks and the next deadline
  • Keep the report pack and the customer advisory
  • Build a CSAF 2.0 oriented file and a dossier PDF in your browser
  • Record who corrected the awareness time

5. Where data lives

  • App data stays in Atlassian Forge Storage and Jira on your site.
  • Atlassian hosts the App. See the Atlassian Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Data remains while the App is installed and your users keep the records. Uninstalling the App removes its Forge Storage for that installation. Jira issues remain under your Jira retention rules.

7. Your rights

Users should ask their Jira administrator for access, correction, or deletion of App data. The organisation may contact the publisher at the email above.
