Security Policy — CRA Advisory for Jira

Last updated: 2026-11-25

This policy covers the Atlassian Forge app CRA Advisory for Jira (“the App”), published by Najafzadeh Reza (egyéni vállalkozó, Hungary).

It describes the security controls, vulnerability management, and incident response for the App. Data handling is in the Privacy Policy.

1. Security controls

  • The App is a native Forge app. It runs on the customer’s Jira site. Atlassian hosts the compute and the storage.
  • The publisher does not run a server for this App. The App has no egress, no analytics, and no public fetch. Customer records are not sent to the publisher.
  • Records stay in Forge Storage on that Jira site, and in Jira. Uninstalling the App removes its Forge Storage for that installation.
  • Scopes are read:jira-work, read:jira-user, and storage:app. The App reads the current issue and Jira user names for the escalation roster. It does not write Jira issues.
  • Who can open the App follows that site’s Jira permissions. The publisher cannot sign in to a customer site.
  • CSAF text and the dossier PDF are built for the user to copy. The App does not send those files anywhere.
  • Customer advisories keep mitigation and affected versions. They leave out exploit detail.
  • The publisher does not claim a certification for the App.

2. Vulnerability management

  • Report a suspected vulnerability to reza@paresh.top with the subject “Security”, or open a request in the support portal.
  • Include the app version, what you observed, and whether customer data could be exposed. Do not include exploit proof-of-concept text.
  • The publisher confirms receipt, decides whether the report affects this App, and ships a fixed version through the Atlassian Marketplace when a change is required.
  • There is no separately hosted service to patch. Fixes ship as a new Marketplace version of this Forge app.
  • There is no bug-bounty program.

3. Incident response

  • An incident is a confirmed weakness in this App that could expose or change customer data, or unauthorized access to the publisher’s Marketplace account.
  • Customer CRA records are stored by Atlassian on the customer site. An incident in Forge or Jira itself is handled by Atlassian under the customer’s agreement with Atlassian.
  • When the publisher confirms a defect in this App, the publisher ships a fixed version and tells affected customers through the support contact on the listing. The notice says what happened and what the customer should do, including updating or uninstalling the App.
  • Public notes and customer advisories do not include exploit detail.
